## Gaps identified (actionable)

### G1 — Predictor regime guard over-bearish in relief rallies ✅ FIXED

When `foreign_sell_streak` + prior crash coincide, guards downgrade banks and
cement to AVOID even if **local/MF bid** is driving a geopolitical relief rally.
**Fix:** Add a "local-bid override" when KSE-100 3d return > +3% OR avg
HIGH-confidence geopolitics/broad-market sentiment > +0.3, cap AVOID count at
50% of universe. (See "Fixes implemented" below — shipped 2026-05-28. Note:
changed AND→OR vs this original prescription so the news leg can catch day-1
before price has bounced.)

### G2 — Cement short thesis lacks rally-regime kill switch ✅ FIXED

Short bucket had no automatic cover when **both** DGKC and KOHC trade above
5-day highs simultaneously. May 19 stops (178.50 / 80.50) were too tight for
Eid squeeze. **Fix:** Hard-cap short conviction to LOW when the name has
rallied >= +8% over the trailing 5 sessions. (See "Fixes implemented" below
— shipped 2026-05-28.)

### G3 — FIPI streak over-weighted vs price action

Strategist deferred bank deploy waiting for FIPI flip; banks rallied **+6.2%**
on local bid anyway. FIPI is necessary for *sustained* foreign-led rally but
should not block **partial** deploy when policy catalysts land (SBP foreign
shareholding reform May 26).

### G4 — Pipeline / data freshness blind spot

9-day gap meant: no EOD scorecard, no prediction refresh, news scoring stale,
health SLA red. Manual runbook (`docs/manual_news_scoring_runbook.md`) worked;
**scheduled workflows cannot run without valid Anthropic key** for predictions,
strategist, and news.

### G5 — KSE-100 index parquet sparse

`kse100.parquet` jumps May 19 → May 28 (missing May 20–25 rows) while symbol
OHLCV has May 25. Index-level backtests and health checks can show **0%**
index return when symbol-level returns are +3–6%.

## Fixes implemented (2026-05-28)

G1 and G2 — the two systematic, code-level failures — are now fixed,
tested, and validated against the live caches. G3 is handled at the
strategist policy layer (today's call already did the partial deploy);
G4/G5 are infra/data items tracked separately.

### G1 — Relief-rally override (`brain/predictor_guards.py`)

`detect_regime` now calls a new `detect_relief_rally(as_of)`. When the
de-risk triggers would fire BUT a local-bid relief rally is in progress,
the regime is forced **off** so the regime-gated guards (A regime cap, C
forecast clamp) stand down. Chase (B) and momentum-exhaustion (D) guards
still run.

A relief rally fires on EITHER (OR, by design — AND can't fire on day-1):

- **News leg (leading):** avg HIGH-confidence **GEOPOLITICS / BROAD_MARKET**
  sentiment `>= +0.30` in the last 48h. Categories are deliberately
  narrow — including MACRO diluted the May-19 signal to +0.25 (bullish
  Iran headlines cancelled by bearish current-account prints) and missed
  day-1. Narrowed, May 19 reads **+0.65**.
- **Index leg (confirming):** trailing 3-session KSE-100 return `>= +3%`.

**Live replay (real caches):** override fires May 19 (news +0.65), May 20
(news), May 21/22/25 (index +3–4%) — the entire rally window. May 18
(crash day) correctly stays **ON**.

Plus a batch backstop `cap_universe_avoid()` wired into
`generate_predictions.py`: if guards push **>50%** of the universe to
AVOID in one batch, the weakest **guard-driven** AVOIDs are relaxed to
WATCH (fundamental AVOIDs are never touched).

### G2 — Short rally kill switch (`brain/short_candidates.py`)

`rank_shorts` now hard-caps a short candidate to **LOW** conviction when
the name has rallied `>= +8%` over the trailing 5 sessions
(`SHORT_RALLY_COVER_5D_PCT`) — the short-side mirror of predictor Guard D.
This is what the May-19 cement shorts (DGKC/KOHC +10%) needed: don't short
into a squeeze. Surfaced as a `rally_kill` field for the UI.
